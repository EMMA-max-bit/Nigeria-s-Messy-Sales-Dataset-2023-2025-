# Nigeria-Messy-Sales-Dataset-2023-2025-
The Nigeria Messy Sales Dataset (2023–2025) contains 550 records and 9 columns, documenting sales transactions from multiple states across Nigeria. Each record includes details such as the customer name, state, product sold, quantity, price, total sale, date, sales channel, and order ID.

## Data Source:
From Kaggle

## Data Cleaning Summary
The data cleaning and preparation process was conducted entirely in Microsoft Excel to ensure data accuracy and consistency. The dataset was carefully reviewed to identify missing values, inconsistent entries, and formatting errors. Missing values in key fields such as Units Sold and Unit Price were filled using product-level averages, while blank Customer Name and Sales Channel entries were replaced with “Unknown.” Inconsistent text formats, such as state and product names, were standardized using proper case, and date values were converted to a uniform format for easy time-based analysis.

Additionally, formulas were used to recalculate missing Total Sale values (=Units Sold * Unit Price) and to extract Day, Month, and Year from the Sale Date column for seasonal trend analysis. Duplicate records and unnecessary rows were removed using Excel’s built-in tools, and conditional formatting was applied to detect and correct anomalies. The cleaned and validated dataset was then saved in the “Cleaned_Data” sheet for further exploration and analysis.

##  Key Analysis and Visuals
The analysis of the Nigeria Messy Sales Dataset (2023–2025) was conducted using Excel Pivot Tables and Excel Charts to uncover key sales trends, product performance, and regional insights. The pivot tables summarized sales across states, products, years, and sales channels, providing a clear view of how sales patterns evolved over time. Charts such as column graphs, line charts, and pie charts were created on the Dashboard sheet to visualize these insights effectively.

From the analysis, Delta, Imo and Kano states emerged as the top-performing regions, contributing the highest share of total sales. Among products, Keyboard and Tablets generated the most revenue, while items like Chargers and Cameras had lower sales figures. The Wholesale channel contributed a larger portion of total revenue compared to online sales, highlighting a stronger market presence in bulk distribution. Seasonal trends indicated noticeable peaks in March and December, suggesting higher consumer demand during festive and promotional periods. Overall, the visuals from the dashboard provide actionable insights for optimizing pricing, inventory, and regional marketing strategies.

##  Insights and Conclusions
The analysis of the Nigeria Sales Dataset (2023–2025) revealed several key insights about product performance, regional demand, and customer behavior. The results showed that Keyboard and Tablets were the top-earning products, while Cameras and Chargers generated lower sales, suggesting opportunities for promotional strategies in those categories. Among sales channels, the Wholesale channel consistently outperformed Online sales, indicating that bulk orders and distributor partnerships were the main drivers of revenue. Regionally, Delta, Imo, and Kano States recorded the highest total sales, highlighting strong market activity in Nigeria’s southern region. Seasonal trends revealed noticeable peaks in March and December, corresponding to festive and high-spending periods.

These findings directly answer the research questions by identifying which products and channels contribute most to revenue and how sales vary across time and regions. The positive relationship observed between unit price, quantity sold, and total revenue suggests that maintaining balanced pricing strategies can enhance profit margins. The analysis also showed that a small but notable percentage of revenue was linked to “Unknown Customers,” emphasizing the need for improved customer data collection. Overall, the insights from the cleaned dataset and Excel visualizations provide a clear understanding of sales dynamics and offer a strong foundation for data-driven decision-making in pricing, marketing, and inventory management.

##  Limitations and Future Work
The main challenge encountered in this analysis was the incomplete and inconsistent nature of the dataset. Several records contained missing values in key fields such as Units Sold, Total Sale, and Customer Name, which required estimation or replacement with “Unidentified” Because the data cleaning was performed entirely in Microsoft Excel, advanced statistical validation and automation were limited compared to using programming tools like Python or Power BI. Additionally, some sales records lacked detailed regional or customer segmentation, which restricted the depth of demographic and behavioral analysis.

For future work, further exploration could involve integrating the dataset with more detailed customer and product data to improve accuracy and insight depth. Using tools such as Python, R, or Power BI would allow for automated data cleaning, predictive modeling, and advanced visualization. Expanding the dataset to include marketing spend, customer demographics, or profit margins would also support more strategic business analysis. These enhancements would strengthen future research and enable more precise recommendations for improving sales performance and resource allocation.
